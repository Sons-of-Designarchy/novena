## Design Context

### Users
Musicians and producers in LATAM (primarily Mexico and Chile) looking to book recording sessions at an independent studio in Colonia Cuauhtémoc, CDMX. They value creative freedom, analog warmth, and authentic craft over corporate polish. They browse the site to get a feel for the space before reaching out — the site is the first impression of the studio's soul.

### Brand Personality
**Three words: warm, raw, confident.**
Chilean roots, CDMX base. Analog film photography, risograph halftone textures, pre-Columbian iconography. Bold graphic identity that doesn't shout — it just stands there and means it. The studio name comes from nine days of intentional preparation. Every design decision should carry that weight.

### Aesthetic Direction
Editorial brutalism warmed up. Think Caldera (condensed display type, extreme sizing, negative letter-spacing, no shadows, surface-color depth) filtered through a Chilean/LATAM analog lens (ivory ground, halftone textures, film grain, earthy terracotta + cobalt palette). Not crypto, not clean SaaS — closer to a music magazine from 1978 that somehow looks current.

**Reference:** Caldera style guide (risograph zine on warm concrete)
**Anti-reference:** Any glossy studio site, any SaaS UI feel, any drop-shadow-heavy card grid

**Theme:** Light. Ivory ground (`#F2EDDF`), near-black text (`#1A1A18`).

### Typography System
- **Display / headings:** HighwayExp (expanded condensed), uppercase, `line-height: 0.88–0.94`, `letter-spacing: -0.02em`. Scale via `clamp()` on marketing pages.
- **Labels / UI / buttons:** Highway, uppercase, `tracking-[0.35em]`, small sizes (12–14px)
- **Body / italic moments:** InstrumentSerif, 16px, `line-height: 1.65`
- **Scale:** 12 / 14 / 16 / 20 / 24 / 30 / 38 / 44 / 56 / 72 / 88px — modular, 1.25× ratio at the base

### Design Principles
1. **Extreme type, quiet everything else.** The headline earns the page. Everything else steps back.
2. **No shadows — depth from surface shifts.** Ivory → sand → cobalt. Printed feel, not glossy.
3. **40px radius system.** Cards and containers use 40px. Buttons use full pill. Nothing uses 8px.
4. **Accent color as currency.** Cobalt and flame appear only where the user must act or something must shout. Not on borders, not on decorative elements.
5. **Photography is the texture.** Halftone treatment bridges the film photo into the graphic system. Don't hide photos — treat them as part of the brand surface.
